Off the Clock / The person behind the work

More than cables,
computers, and error codes.

The work is a big part of my story. It isn't the whole thing. There's family, music, some questionable humor, and usually another idea I can't quite leave alone.

01 / The people who matter

Family is the good stuff.

I'm a family man with two kids and two bonus kids. That part of my life means a great deal to me, and it reminds me that the best connections don't run through a cable.

I'm keeping this page personal without turning my family's private lives into public information.

Four kids. A full heart. Probably not enough hours in the day.

02 / Karaoke has a way of bringing people together

Yes, there's a microphone involved.

Karaoke has been part of my world for years. I ran Addicted to Karaoke in Phoenix back in the late 2000s, and I've been working on bringing that community idea back—helping singers, hosts, and venues find one another.

For me, karaoke isn't only about the song. It's about the people who show up, cheer each other on, and give the person at the microphone a reason to smile.

The music

A good night can turn complete strangers into a chorus. Even if nobody quite remembers the second verse.

The building

I enjoy taking an idea, figuring out how it could help people, and seeing if I can make it useful in the real world.

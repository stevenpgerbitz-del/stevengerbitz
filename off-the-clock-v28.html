01 / The people who matter

Family is the good stuff.

I'm a proud dad and bonus dad to four young adults. Family means a great deal to me, and it reminds me that the best connections don't run through a cable.

I'm keeping this page personal without turning my family's private lives into public information.

Four young adults. A full heart. Probably not enough hours in the day.

02 / Karaoke has a way of bringing people together

Yes, there's a microphone involved.

Karaoke isn't just something I enjoy singing. I used to own my own equipment and run my own karaoke shows. And I'm talking about the CD+G and LaserDisc days—long before you could pull up just about any song with a few taps.

I had hundreds of CDs and LaserDiscs to keep track of, and I even put together my own songbooks so people could flip through the choices. Adding new music meant updating the books, not just refreshing a screen. Those were the days!

Now karaoke libraries can be searched in seconds, and songs that once took some hunting are practically available on a whim. The technology has changed, but my favorite part hasn't: the people who show up, cheer each other on, and give someone at the microphone a reason to smile.

I also ran Addicted to Karaoke in Phoenix back in the late 2000s, and I'm bringing that community idea back to help singers, hosts, and venues find one another.

The music

From flipping through handwritten songbooks to searching a digital library, a good night can still turn complete strangers into a chorus. Even if nobody remembers the second verse.

The building

Whether it's putting together a karaoke show or bringing a community idea back to life, I enjoy figuring out how the pieces fit together—and making it work for people.

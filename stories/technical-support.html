02 / Technical Support / The story

I like a good puzzle.

I enjoy figuring out what's wrong. But the part I like most is helping someone get past a problem that's been making their day harder.

A little background

The part that makes the job worthwhile.

One of the things I've always enjoyed about technical support is helping people solve problems that are making their day more difficult.

Technology can be frustrating, especially when something isn't working the way it should. By the time someone asks for help, they've often already spent time trying to figure it out themselves.

I enjoy the challenge of finding out what's wrong and getting things working again. But what makes it rewarding is seeing the relief on someone's face when the problem is finally solved.

Sometimes it's a complicated issue that takes a lot of troubleshooting. Other times, it's something surprisingly simple. Either way, knowing I've made someone's day a little easier is what I enjoy most about technical support.

Sometimes the equipment isn't broken. You just need a different way to accomplish what the person is trying to do.

Start with what's actually happening.

I usually have a mental checklist. If a monitor isn't working, for example, I'll check the power, the connections, and the computer before assuming a major repair is needed. That approach works whether I'm dealing with a classroom computer, a piece of hardware, or a fiber connection.

I enjoy working through the possibilities, and I'm comfortable changing direction when an approach isn't working. Sometimes, when the situation allows, it helps to come back to a problem with a fresh perspective.

Leave useful notes behind.

Documentation matters to me. I want the original issue, what I checked, and the final resolution recorded so another technician doesn't have to start over. If I find a good solution, I'll share it verbally or write it down.

As I like to say, “If you didn't document it, it never happened.”

The work behind the words

Windows troubleshooting, hardware repairs, device setup, Microsoft Intune, Mosyle MDM, Active Directory support, diagnostics, and clear ticket documentation.

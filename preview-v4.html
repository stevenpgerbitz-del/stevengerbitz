01 / The person

There's more to a person than a résumé.

I'm a technology guy at heart. I like understanding how things work, figuring out what went wrong, and helping people get back to what they were trying to do in the first place.

I've had the chance to do that in all kinds of places—schools, mines, businesses, homes, and more remote communications sites than I could probably count.

The job titles have changed over the years, but my favorite part hasn't: there's always something new to learn, and usually somebody you can help along the way.

*Good work matters. So does being someone people actually enjoy working with.

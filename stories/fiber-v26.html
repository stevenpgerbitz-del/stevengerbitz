01 / Fiber Optics / The story

Getting the connection right.

A good fiber installation isn't just about seeing light at the other end. It's about the care that goes into everything between those two points.

A little background

The part you don't always see.

Fiber work has taken me into homes, businesses, service trucks, roadside pedestals, and some interesting corners of the country. At Vantage Point Solutions, I worked on installations across 27 states. Later, at MetroNet, I was tracing service problems and replacing damaged customer drops.

There's something satisfying about the details: a clean route for the cable, properly arranged fibers, a good splice, and test results that confirm what your hands have done. I like knowing that the work behind the cover is just as good as what someone can see.

It's not always glamorous. Sometimes you're out in the weather trying to work out where a connection went bad. But the challenge is part of what makes the solution worthwhile.

“Just because it works doesn't mean it's right.”

What it means to me

To me, fiber optics represents craftsmanship and pride in doing a job properly. Getting the service working matters, of course. So does leaving behind work that another technician can understand and trust.

I also enjoyed helping newer technicians learn the trade on actual jobs. Over time, I worked one-on-one with approximately 15 new fiber installers. Showing someone how to solve a problem—and then watching them figure out the next one for themselves—is a pretty good feeling.

The work behind the words

Fiber installation, fusion splicing, OTDR and optical testing, customer-premises equipment, documentation, and hands-on field mentoring.

The story

From both sides of the microphone.

I started building websites in 1998, and my karaoke adventure began in 1999 as both a singer and karaoke host. In 2007, I founded Addicted to Karaoke in the Phoenix area. In 2026, I brought the project back to life with modern web technologies and AI-assisted development.

What it does

Addicted to Karaoke (A2K) is a community-driven platform connecting singers, karaoke hosts (KJs), and venues.

  • Discover karaoke venues, events and schedules.
  • Explore member profiles and venue and host reviews.
  • Submit listings and schedules for community moderation.
  • Manage membership, approvals, security and database recovery through administrative tools.

Behind the scenes

I shape the product requirements, workflows, design direction and testing, working collaboratively with AI-assisted development tools. The project uses HTML, CSS, JavaScript, GitHub Pages, Supabase and PostgreSQL.

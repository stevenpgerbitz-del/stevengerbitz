03 / What I bring to the table

A little curiosity goes a long way.

I've spent more than 20 years working with technology in all sorts of places: classrooms, mines, homes, businesses, and some pretty remote communications sites.

I've been the person answering the phone, the one standing in front of the equipment, and the one showing a newer technician how to get a job done right.

06 / Mission & values

What I stand for.

The person I am at work and the person I am away from it shouldn't be two different people. I try to bring the same values to both.

01 / IN MY WORK

Fix the problem.
Respect the person.

My mission is to make technology less frustrating and more useful for the people who depend on it. That means being curious before making assumptions, explaining things without talking down to anyone, and taking ownership until the job is done right.

  • Listen first. Understand the problem before chasing a solution.
  • Do it right. Be thorough, honest, and willing to learn.
  • Leave people better off. A good fix includes the person on the other end.

02 / IN MY LIFE

Show up.
Be someone people can count on.

Family, kindness, and community matter to me. Being a dad to two kids and two bonus kids has a way of keeping things in perspective. I don't have all the answers, and I don't need to make a big speech about what I believe. I'd rather try to show it in how I treat people.

  • Make time for people. Especially the ones closest to me.
  • Keep a sense of humor. Life is serious enough without making every moment serious.
  • Give people a chance. A little patience can go a long way.

Some principles have stayed with me, including the Air Force values of Integrity First, Service Before Self, and Excellence In All We Do.

“Doing the right thing is never the wrong thing.” — Ted Lasso

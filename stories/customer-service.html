03 / Customer Service / The story

There's a person on the other side.

A technical issue might be routine to the person fixing it. It might be the thing that's holding up somebody else's entire day.

A little background

Technology is only half the conversation.

I've spent a lot of my career working directly with people. Sometimes that meant talking someone through a broadband connection over the phone. Other times it meant being at their home, their business, or their school, trying to get something working again.

The technical side matters, but so does how you talk to someone while you're working on it. People don't need to feel foolish because they don't know a networking term or where a particular setting lives. They need somebody who listens, explains what is happening, and follows through.

I've worked in broadband support, hosting support, onsite IT, fiber installation, and schools. Those jobs may look different on a résumé, but they all have that part in common.

“Good connections are about more than cables.”

What it means to me

Customer service, to me, is about respect. It's remembering that every ticket or outage has a real person attached to it.

I like being able to solve the problem and leave someone feeling heard—not brushed aside. And when the moment is right, a little humor can make the whole experience easier. The serious part is knowing when that moment is.

The work behind the words

One-on-one support, clear explanations, patient troubleshooting, onsite service, practical guidance, and respectful follow-through.
